A Legacy of Transformation

From India to the World — Leading Excellence Transformations

2017

Foundation Year

Global Corporate Council India (GCCI) was established in Pune, India, with a mission to drive business excellence, TPM, TQM, and Lean-based transformations across industries.

2018

Formation of TPM Consulting Division

GCCI formally structured its TPM (Total Productive Maintenance) and Operational Excellence Consulting Division, initiating partnerships with major Indian manufacturing organizations.

2019

Launch of Capability Development & Assessment Services

Introduced TPM Health Check-Up Assessments, Capability Development Programs, and Business Excellence Training Modules for organizations across India.

2020

Global Collaboration and Technology Integration

GCCI expanded globally by initiating strategic partnerships with overseas experts and organizations for AI-based, IoT, and automation-driven TPM solutions.

2021

Digital TPM and Visual Management Innovation

Developed Visual Management Systems (VMS) and introduced digital TPM frameworks integrating smart manufacturing concepts.

2022

Global Recognition and Expansion

GCCI achieved recognition for its contribution to 69+ JIPM TPM Awards through consulting support and collaboration with multiple industries.

2023

Rebranding and Globalization

Rebranded as GCCI International LLP, reflecting a global consulting vision with operations and collaborations across Japan, Germany, the U.S., and Australia.

2024

Institutionalization of GCCI Learning & Certification

"Initiated plans for GCCI TPM Academy, focusing on TPM, TQM, RCM, and Business Excellence certifications in collaboration with academic and industrial partners.

2025

Digital Transformation & AI Integration

Launched initiatives for AI-supported TPM deployment, digital dashboards, and data-driven assessment methodologies to align with Industry 4.0 and Smart Factory frameworks.
